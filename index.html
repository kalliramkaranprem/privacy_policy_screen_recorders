🔒 Executive Privacy Summary

ScreenCast Pro operates under a Strict Local-Only Architecture. All screen captures, audio inputs, Facecam camera streams, and video recordings are processed and encoded entirely on your local device. We do NOT collect, upload, stream, sell, or transmit any video footage, microphone audio, or personal identification to remote cloud servers or third parties.

1 Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ScreenCast Pro ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles user information and permissions when you install and use the application on mobile devices, tablets, and web platforms.

We strictly comply with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy, Device and Network Abuse Policy, Permissions and Sensitive APIs Policy, and Foreground Services Policy.

2 Sensitive Permissions & Device Data Handled

To perform high-definition screen recording and video authoring, ScreenCast Pro requests specific hardware and system permissions. Below is an exhaustive breakdown of every sensitive permission requested, its exact purpose, and our technical safeguard:

Permission / API Platform Capability Specific Purpose Data Handling & Retention
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION / getDisplayMedia Screen & Display Capture Captures user-selected mobile screen, app, or gameplay video frames at requested resolutions (4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p). Requires explicit user confirmation for each session. Encoded directly into local MP4 file chunks. Never transmitted off-device.
RECORD_AUDIO / getUserMedia (audio) Microphone & Audio Input Used solely when user selects "Microphone" or "Mic + System Audio" to record voice commentary over screen footage. Mixed in real-time into the video audio stream via Web Audio API. No ambient or background audio surveillance is ever performed.
CAMERA / getUserMedia (video) Front / Rear Camera Powers the optional Facecam Picture-in-Picture (PIP) overlay on the screen recording. Rendered strictly in local real-time memory or composited onto the video canvas. No biometric or facial recognition data is extracted or stored.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / IndexedDB Device Local Storage Saves exported High Definition MP4 video files, thumbnails, and screenshot stills to local device storage, and loads saved clips for in-app playback and trimming. Files remain in user's isolated local device storage. User retains 100% control to export, move, or delete files at any time.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Floating Overlay Bubble Displays floating recording pill/controls (Pause, Resume, Stop, Brush, Screenshot) over other apps during screen recording. Provides UI control affordance only. Does not inspect or log contents of other apps.
VIBRATE / Sensor Haptic Feedback & Shake Detector Provides tactile feedback and allows optional "Shake device to stop recording" trigger. Sensor values are evaluated in volatile RAM in real-time and immediately discarded.

3 Data Collection, Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Collected: We do not require account registration, passwords, names, phone numbers, or email addresses to use the core screen recording functionality.
  • No Sale or Monetization of User Data: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your video recordings, personal metadata, or device information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Tracking SDKs: The application does not bundle invasive profiling, user tracking, or behavioral ad targeting libraries.
  • No Cloud Upload: Your recorded videos, audio tracks, and annotations are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud infrastructure without your explicit manual sharing action.

4 Data Storage, Retention & Deletion Rights

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device (via browser IndexedDB and device file downloads):

  • Instant Deletion: You can delete any recording permanently at any time by tapping the trash icon in the application's Videos (Gallery) tab.
  • Clear All Data: The application provides a one-tap "Clear Cached Recordings" button in the Settings tab which permanently purges all stored media from local device storage.
  • App Uninstall / Storage Clear: Uninstalling the application or clearing browser/application storage through your operating system settings will completely and permanently erase all stored recordings and configuration settings.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

ScreenCast Pro is a general utility tool designed for productivity, tutorial authoring, and mobile gaming capture. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in applicable European Union jurisdictions), in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6 Technical Security Safeguards

Because ScreenCast Pro does not store user data on remote databases or transmission pipes, the primary attack surfaces associated with cloud data breaches are eliminated. On-device media is sandboxed within standard operating system security boundaries (Android app sandbox / browser origin security policy).
